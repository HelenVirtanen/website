Moscow CSS is a professional community of interface developers, mostly on the web, who love CSS, browser technology and design. We meet regularly to talk about interfaces: how the browser works under the hood, how it computes transforms, how to find critical CSS and dead styles, how to build a design system and not hate it six months later. We look at how a user journey takes shape and how visuals change the way people perceive an app. Sometimes we step aside to talk about motivation, judging your own level, freelancing and learning fast: a good developer grows as a whole.

The community has been around since 2015, and the meetups since 2017. More than 85 speakers have given talks, and about 5,000 people have come to the meetups. Admission is free, in the room in Moscow or online.
