Spring HolyJS is over. We were an invited community there and ran activities for the guests. We prepared two games.

## CSS Rescue Squad

An interactive Git quiz for front-end developers. There were many guests, so at the stand you needed 7 points: one hard task or a mix of easy and medium ones. If you got stuck there were hints, and after that links to the walkthrough and the documentation. Many people came back just to solve more.

The code is open: suggest new tasks, improve the texts, add sign-up and a leaderboard, or take the game to your own event.

## Defend the project's tech stack

The hardest mode, “Chaos”, gives you 5 minutes and 12 technologies. A team of five or more has to convince the host why they need Python, Go and Bun on the back end and Next, Nuxt, Solid and Remix on the front end, all in one project. Once a React stack got a lone Pinia, and the team got away with it: the project was a Vue.js learning platform. A few times the stack came out perfectly sensible, and then it was the hosts who were sweating. Some guests even took over as hosts and ran a few games themselves.

## New faces

Alyona Laskina and Elena Virtanen were at the stand for the first time, and Darina Pairel joined the team too. They help make new meetups, come up with activities and prepare speakers.

Thanks to JUG Ru Group for this front-end celebration and for letting us be part of it!
