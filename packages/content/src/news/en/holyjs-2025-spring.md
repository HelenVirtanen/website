For the first time the organizers of the HolyJS JavaScript conference invited JS communities to their expo, and MoscowCSS said yes. On 7–8 April in Moscow we had our own stand: sharing knowledge, running giveaways and handing out merch.

Together with the other communities the stand ran a game of Family Feud. The answers came from an anonymous survey of our channel's subscribers, who could answer seriously or for fun.

The MoscowCSS programme committee survived. It was great and busy, lots of people came by the stand. We played Grid Garden, and someone learned to use grids from scratch. Together with friends we gave away presents and merch.
