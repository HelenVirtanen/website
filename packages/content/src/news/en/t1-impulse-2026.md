On 19 November the T1 IT holding runs T1 Impulse for the fifth time, an applied IT conference with real cases and the chance to talk in person to colleagues from many companies. Last year it had more than 5,000 attendees and more than 130 engineers on stage.

MoscowCSS will be there with its own stand, come and say hi!

The programme has five tracks: architecture and design, product vision and domain development, DevOps and SDLC, design and user experience, teamwork and processes. There are many formats: talks, workshops, labs and game simulations. The talks were selected by a programme committee from T1, Yandex, Sber, Ozon and community leaders, MoscowCSS among them.

Attendance is free for guests.
