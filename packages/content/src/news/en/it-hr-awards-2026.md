MoscowCSS is taking part in the IT HR Awards in the “IT communities” category.

Your reviews will help us a lot. If you spoke at a meetup, came to listen, or helped with a venue or the organization, tell us how you know MoscowCSS. A couple of paragraphs in your own words, a photo is welcome.

How to leave a review:

- send a direct message to [@DreamShaded](https://t.me/DreamShaded) on Telegram, and we will format and publish it;
- or send a pull request to the [website repository](https://github.com/moscowcss-meetup/website): the steps are in the README, section “Оставить отзыв” (leave a review).

All reviews live on the [reviews page](https://css.moscow/en/reviews/). Thank you!
