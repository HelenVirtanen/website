It is hard to imagine a good modern website without animation. You often have to rack your brain over implementing a designer's idea. How do you get the result you want without exhausting yourself? Which approach should you choose: pure CSS, changing styles through JS, or is it time to bring in Canvas? What will run faster? Where can you look at performance? I will try to answer all these questions and demonstrate the advantages of the different approaches.
