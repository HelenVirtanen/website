I often come across the belief that a markup developer does not have to understand web design or graphics software, or show excessive creativity. My task is to prove the opposite. With examples, I will show how to squeeze everything you need out of graphics programs, talk about excessive communication with specialists when working in a team, how to learn web design while working as a markup developer, and why a good markup developer is much more important and valuable than a good web designer.
