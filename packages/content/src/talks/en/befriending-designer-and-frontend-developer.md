A lot of time has passed since life split web masters into designers and markup developers. At first we did not understand each other at all, but modern tools have reduced the distance between us and keep doing so. We began to use similar technologies and the same terms, and sometimes even to get by without each other.

Let us talk about how to get even closer and learn to speak the same language.
