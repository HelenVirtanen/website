We will answer the question of why the structure and the design of slides are inseparable. A presentation as a product. What to do if you are not a designer but need slides? At the meetup we will go through many pressing questions: "Where do I start?", "How do I estimate resources?", "What questions should I ask the venue organizers to avoid surprises?", "What are the main steps in preparing a presentation structure?". We will answer when and why slides are needed, and look at different kinds of presentations and how they differ. Visualization in a presentation.
