I am convinced that a successful developer is not only someone who knows and can do a lot, but also someone who can organize their workflow well and efficiently. Over my years in development I have accumulated plenty of such experience, and I think it may be useful to someone. I will talk about how I plan and control my work tasks, the role time management plays in it, and share workhacks from my own experience.
