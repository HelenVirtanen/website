Courses say salaries in IT are high. Companies write that there is a shortage of IT specialists. But if you are only just entering IT, reality may turn out to be less rosy. How do you build relationships in a new team to avoid hazing and abuse? I will talk about typical mistakes that you can use to dig yourself a pit. And I will give practical tools for avoiding them
