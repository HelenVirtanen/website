What sets a strong markup developer apart from an ordinary one? Knowing new technologies, work experience, strong muscles? No, the main difference is the ability to pick the right CSS methodology. Let us figure out what a CSS methodology is, split the audience into two camps, look at several of the most popular methodologies, divide them into three main types and learn how to cross them properly.
