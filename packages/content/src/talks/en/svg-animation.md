SVG is not only an icon for your application but a powerful tool that can add captivating animations that amaze the imagination. In our talk we will go through various methods of animating SVG graphics and share recommendations on choosing the most suitable one. You will also learn a few interesting tricks that will add originality to your projects. Join us and discover the world of stunning SVG animations!
