title: "Dopamine in interfaces: how the urge to come back works"
---

You drop in to look at one product and spend half an hour scrolling recommendations. You finish a step and want to complete the whole progress bar. You get a reward and come back for the next one.

Behind these familiar scenarios are specific product decisions that designers and developers make together every day: how to show progress, when to give feedback, how to mark success and what to offer next.

Using examples from familiar apps, we'll break down engagement mechanics, the behaviour they support and why products need them.

We'll discuss how to use this understanding when frontend and design work together: seeing the meaning behind individual interface elements, asking precise questions about features and creating scenarios that are a pleasure to use.
