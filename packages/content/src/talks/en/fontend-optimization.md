Unfortunately, no ideal way of loading custom fonts has been invented yet. As a result, a huge amount of time is spent searching for best practices. In this talk you will learn which font formats you can say goodbye to, how to deal with text transparency and flashing, and whether you should remove unneeded glyphs from a font and convert it to base64.
