I have been writing code in the cozy macOS for 6 years. Crisp pictures, rich colors on a Retina screen, convenient tools, Sketch among them: developing is so pleasant! The paradox is that most of the people who use the products I write code for are on Windows. So Windows-specific bugs can easily slip past a developer. Never mind the bugs: the close button of a popup should be on the left! Or is it on the right after all?
