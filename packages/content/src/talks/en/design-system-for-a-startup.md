At Vectorly we thought about a design system almost at the very start of the product, but we did not know where to begin, or whether to bother at all. The examples we saw in the open were design systems of big companies, so it was hard for us to apply their experience to a startup. Using our product as an example, we want to explain why a startup needs a design system, how to get started and move on, and how many people it takes.
