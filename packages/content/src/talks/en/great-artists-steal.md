Modern CSS/HTML is very complex. Grid, SubGrid, Flexbox, CSS Regions: all of it takes skill, experience and patience. What do you do if you have none of that in your arsenal yet, or if you need something "just like that one, but by yesterday"? Can you somehow use someone else's work and not redo what has already been done? Yes, you can. In this talk I will teach you what nobody else will teach you: how to steal markup.
