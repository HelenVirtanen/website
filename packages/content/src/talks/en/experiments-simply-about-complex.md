How do you tell which changes to your service are worth keeping, and which are better hidden from everyone?

How do you blow up an interface without scaring users away?

Green or red button, Neo?

At a high level, without digging into details, we will try to answer the most important questions: what experiments are, what kinds there are, whether they have implementation specifics and, most importantly, what they can give us.
