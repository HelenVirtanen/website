title: "Through thorns to the ATM"

In August 2018, Tinkoff Bank launched its own ATM network. One of the tasks facing the team was developing an original, clear and convenient interface for customers. A story about why it matters to choose technologies based on the conditions, what problems we ran into, how they could have been avoided, and how it all turned out.
