In markup, good practice is reached by rewriting your own code many times over. We will cut that time short. Inspired by other people's sad experience, we will go through 15 of your CSS mistakes.
You will learn what lies at the foundation of correct approaches, methods for diagnosing bad practice, and ways to rewrite incorrect CSS. You will get 15 real code examples before and after optimization. Let's go!
