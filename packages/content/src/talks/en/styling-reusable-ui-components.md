One of the key properties of universal, reusable components is customizable styles. Adding a couple of extra CSS properties sometimes turns out to be deceptively simple. How do you stay flexible without losing the consistency of the site design? What practical use can the SOLID and KISS principles have for your markup? In my talk I will try to answer these and other interesting questions.
