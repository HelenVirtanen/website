For several years the portal design team at Mail.Ru Group has been updating and unifying its products. We have built a design system that powers the media projects, the mobile web and partly the productivity services (other products are being connected gradually), along with a style of icons and illustrations, and we are standardizing promo emails and promo sites. Of course, not every service is in good shape yet, and in some places the first redesign did not solve every problem, but the huge leap of recent years is hard to miss. To speed up the update process and make our work public, we opened part of our Paradigm design system to the outside. I will talk about its new architecture, which we are announcing this spring.
