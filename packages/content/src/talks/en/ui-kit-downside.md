As a rule, problems arise when customers interfere in an area where they have no competent experience and thereby cause markup problems. You will learn a way to relatively safely create skins for a platform into which new widgets and fundamental changes to the BS3 markup have been introduced, and also to give the customer a choice: do you want the looks or the ride?
