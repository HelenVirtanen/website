Imagine you have a page in a new microblogging service from a company called Troodle. You have gathered a few thousand followers and keep filling the microblog with content. And at some point Troodle decides to shut the service down without preserving your posts, because of a business pivot. Did you know there is a whole set of specifications that makes your content truly yours? In the talk I will show what the IndieWeb initiative includes and how to implement its ideas on your own site.
