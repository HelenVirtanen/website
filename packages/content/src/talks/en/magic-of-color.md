In this talk we will follow the path color takes from bits in video memory to our consciousness, the transformations it goes through on the way, and why it matters not only for designers but for interface developers. We will also learn how to control color and make it your helper when designing interfaces.

After the theoretical part we will look at various color tools and use an example to see how to apply the knowledge in projects
