Our pages keep getting heavier, and given modern trends the situation will only get worse. It is time to think not only about beauty but also about optimization. Content plays a major role here, specifically the images we serve to the client. We will talk about the advantages the WebP image format gives us, the state of its support, and how to use WebP as effectively as possible today.
