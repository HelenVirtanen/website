Many companies that do project-based development sooner or later face the need to create shared bases of frontend components that can be reused across different projects. This not only saves time but also lets you build projects with fewer bugs. In my talk I would like to explain how our company adopted this approach, what difficulties we met along the way and which principles we followed.
