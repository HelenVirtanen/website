A user opens your site on a mobile device on a tram. After opening the site, the user patiently waits until they can interact with it. Still waiting. And still waiting. And then their stop comes. And the site is still loading. Why does this happen? It is simple: every year the size of our bundles grows, and so does the number of active mobile users. Sooner or later all this will make you think about the performance of your site, preferably before conversion drops and new gray hairs appear. I would like to discuss with you how to make the users of your sites a little happier. I will tell you how we tackled our performance issues and what it gave us in the end. Of course, without rocket science (but that is not certain).
