We all love to learn. There is even a syndrome for it: the eternal student. But how do you find the balance, so you neither study for too long nor start working too early? I will talk about my learning experience and the moment I started taking my first orders. I will also cover the differences I saw between studying and working, and how this experience helps me keep growing.
