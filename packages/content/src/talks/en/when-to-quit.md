"Every Sunday I realize with horror that tomorrow is a workday." Sound familiar? It seems each of us has been in a similar situation. But far from everyone stopped to think at that moment that maybe the problem is not them. "I am surrounded by good specialists, I just cannot communicate": when there is no dialogue in the team, or there is outright conflict. And an endless search for a solution that leads to a dead end. We will talk about this and much more at the meetup. We will make a small checklist and think: what if it is time to quit?
