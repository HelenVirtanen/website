title: "Design without a designer: basic principles that help a developer build a site that does not kill conversion"

A talk about how a developer can make an interface clear and neat even without a designer. We go through simple but critically important rules: page structure, grid and rhythm, working with fonts, colors and visuals. Only logic and practical techniques that save time and spare you rework.
