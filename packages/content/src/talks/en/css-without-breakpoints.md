title: "CSS without breakpoints: responsive interfaces with container queries, :has() and cascade layers"
---

Forget about breakpoints: interfaces adapt on their own with `container queries`, so components respond to the size of their container, not the screen. You will learn how the `:has()` pseudo-class and cascade layers bring order to priorities, making CSS predictable and scalable.
