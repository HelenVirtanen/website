Moscow CSS is a community of frontend developers who love CSS and layout. Since 2 March 2017 we have held more than thirty meetups: talks about native CSS features, design systems, animation, accessibility, typography and frontend tools, and also about how a developer grows and works with designers.

## How the meetups work

A meetup is an evening of several talks, questions to the speakers and networking afterwards. Entry is free, attending in person needs a registration, and many meetups can be watched online. Companies host the meetups in their offices: over the years we have been hosted by Yandex, Rambler, Tinkoff, Raiffeisenbank, Ozon, Cian, Kaspersky, MTS AI, Skillbox, Avito, red_mad_robot, MTS Fintech, School 21, Zvuk and others.

## Recordings and the archive

The programme of every meetup, its talks and speakers are on this site: start with the [list of past meetups](https://css.moscow/en/#events) or the [search](https://css.moscow/en/search/). Talk recordings are on the [Moscow CSS YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Moscowcss) and on VK Video; the links are on the meetup and talk pages.

## How to take part

New meetups are announced in the [Telegram channel @moscowcss](https://t.me/moscowcss). Want to give a talk? Write to the organisers, their contacts are on the [Contact](https://css.moscow/en/contact/) page.
