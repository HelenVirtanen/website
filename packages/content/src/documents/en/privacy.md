## Definitions

1. Program committee (PC): the administrators of the channel [@moscowcss](https://t.me/moscowcss), listed in the channel description
2. Participant: a legally capable individual who wishes to speak at the conference
3. Personal data: last name, first name, and a contact: a phone number or a link to a social network
4. Meetup: a gathering of participants with prepared talks and of listeners

## Policy and rules

1. The participant provides personal data in person to the MoscowCSS meetup program committee before the talk is approved for the program of the nearest meetup. The data is not passed on to third parties.
2. Personal data is processed to put together the meetup program and to contact the prospective speaker.
3. After approval, personal data may be passed on to the partner organization of the conference, on whose premises the meetup will be held.
4. Once the partner is chosen, the MoscowCSS PC will ask you for consent again.
5. The data is provided for a period of at least 25 years and can be withdrawn by the applicant by contacting the channel [@moscowcss](https://t.me/moscowcss).
