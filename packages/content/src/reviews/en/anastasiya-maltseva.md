I'm a developer and a speaker, with more than 30 talks in Russia and abroad. And it all began with a Moscow CSS meetup, where I gave one of my first talks.

When the organisers invited me, I had doubts: can I really do this, won't I forget every word in existence, won't I be pelted with technical questions by people with 60 years of experience who write in every language in the world.

But I agreed 😄

On the day of the meetup I lost my appetite and considered fleeing the country) but the organisers are great people and I couldn't let them down. So I went.

I was the second speaker, and throughout the first talk I was frantically trying to remember my material, even though I'd run through it about 10 times at home, introducing everyone in the household, including the aloe on the windowsill, to the finer points of layout)

I walked on stage, Roma and Egor introduced me, I took the microphone and looked out at the room. My friend Lera was sitting in the front row, smiling and waving) in the third row a couple of people looked up from their phones and nodded approvingly.

And at that moment I felt the kind of rush you get after a parachute jump or a hard exam) and the words flowed, and the jokes landed, and the audience responded to my questions and activities)) and I felt that rush, and very powerful support from the room

And to this day, after all my talks, I still remember with special warmth that Moscow CSS where it all began ❤️
