I come to MoscowCSS all the time, and over time I started helping to find speakers. What I like most is watching people step on stage for the first time. At first they are nervous, but after a couple of talks they speak with confidence, give talks more often and start inviting others.

I also like that the organizers broaden the audience, not only deepen it: besides front-end, you can learn about design, security, copyright and much more. The community brings acquaintances that later help at work: I have seen people get promoted after their talks.

For me MoscowCSS is a place where people grow. A real forge of speakers)
