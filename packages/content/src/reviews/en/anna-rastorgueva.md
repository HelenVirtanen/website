When I first came to MoscowCSS, I immediately felt it was a very friendly place. It was the first community that accepted me, and that's where I gave my first talk. The team helped me prepare, suggested how to structure the material properly, and even taught me how to carry myself on stage. They are very kind and responsive in general, always ready to support you. At the meetups I met loads of interesting people, both from my profession and from related fields, so now I have people to discuss not only work with but other things too. But most importantly, after MoscowCSS I came to believe in myself: I realised I'm a good speaker and that I should definitely keep doing this.
