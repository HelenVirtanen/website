I first came to MoscowCSS about three years ago, when I was just starting out in IT. Back then I went to meetups looking for new knowledge, experience and useful contacts. Over time I realised that what I value most at such events is the people. Thanks to MoscowCSS I met people I now see not only at meetups but in everyday life. Another nice bonus is the chance to visit the offices of different IT companies, see how they work and meet potential employers. Now I come to MoscowCSS not only for the talks but also to meet people I already know well and always enjoy spending time with.
