Not long ago I went to a wonderful IT event, MoscowCSS, at School 21. I burst into this frontend gathering thanks to my friend Roman from Sber. My respect, Roman 🎩

Roma and I had long planned to tell his colleagues about intellectual property. Roma collected about 40 questions from IT colleagues, we picked the main themes, exchanged a lot of long messages, and the first talk was ready.

I talked about works made for hire and copyright infringement, using memes as examples. I held forth for almost a whole hour, and then there were more than 20 questions in the chat and in person, which I answered at the afterparty with the IT audience in a bar. Yes, I was drinking beer and explaining photo rights 😉

I should say that people in IT get their bearings in law quite quickly and well; many dig into all the statutes and court practice on their own.

I also liked the deep, well-thought-out questions from my IT comrades: no retelling of rumours or Granny Lyuba's opinions, but a reliance on facts and practice.

😎Long story short, I liked it, and I'll go again! 😎 If they invite me, of course.
