2017 had only just begun when the folks from Saint Petersburg brought the frontend meetup format to Moscow and handed us everything literally on a silver platter. It was pleasant, interesting and absorbing: all that remained was not to stop.

Moscow always lives at a fast pace: everyone is doing something, everyone has good ideas, and almost nobody has the time to make them happen. And the timing for starting MoscowCSS was excellent!

That's how the first years of MoscowCSS flew by: new venues, the first difficulties, talks at the crossroads of development and design, sometimes on quite experimental topics. And many great people from MoscowJS and other communities, thanks to whom it all found its voice and character.

I'd like to say those were the golden years. Although, of course, everyone has their own.
