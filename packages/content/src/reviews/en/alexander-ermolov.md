For me, MoscowCSS is much more than just conferences. For me, it's Roman Troitsky and a community of like-minded people where you can share experience, find inspiration and meet people who genuinely want to grow in their field.

Over the time I've been involved, I've not only found kindred spirits but also became part of the programme myself and tried my hand as a speaker.

It gave me the chance to share my own experience, look at familiar things from a new angle and step outside everyday tasks.
