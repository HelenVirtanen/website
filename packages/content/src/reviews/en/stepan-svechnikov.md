I first came to MoscowCSS about three years ago and since then I try not to miss a single meetup!

I especially like talks about non-obvious approaches to problems and about where CSS is heading. During the talks I keep catching myself thinking: “How could I use this in my project?” Of course, in enterprise development not everything can be adopted, but when I manage to put something I heard into practice, it's a special pleasure :)

And of course the atmosphere deserves a mention. The meetups are always intimate, cosy and truly heartfelt. Special thanks for that to the organizers and the whole MoscowCSS community!
